About

From an idea to something people can walk into.

Amal Alsofayan is a Creative Marketing Specialist based in Riyadh who works across the full distance between a concept and its execution.

That means she moves between developing the idea itself, shaping the content strategy around it, writing the words and scripts that carry it, directing the shoot or set that brings it to life, and staying close enough to the digital side — Meta, Google, TikTok, and Snapchat campaigns — to know how an idea should actually travel once it's out in the world.

What sets her approach apart

  1. She stays hands-on past the idea stage

    Creative direction for Amal doesn't stop at a concept deck — it continues onto set: moodboards, styling, model coordination, and directing photographers and production days in person.

  2. She thinks in campaigns, not single posts

    Her work connects an idea to a strategy, a piece of content to a channel, and a single activation to the broader brand experience around it.

  3. She knows the digital side well enough not to over-rely on it

    Hands-on experience with Meta, Google, TikTok, and Snapchat campaigns means she can connect creative work to the right audience and objective — without letting digital execution become the whole story.
